# KiviDesign

Kivi is a local-first voice interface that gets more yours the longer you use it.

## Live Prototype

[Open the live Kivi prototype](https://sanjeetkotarya.github.io/KiviDesign/)

## Product Direction

Kivi's **Imprints** are per-app voice profiles formed from real corrections over time. They are not presets or settings: Kivi starts as a stranger, learns how you actually speak in each context, and keeps your language switching and tone close to your intent without rewriting what you mean.
